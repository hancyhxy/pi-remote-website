    Not end-to-end encrypted.

    Claude and ChatGPT connect from their own cloud, so their tool calls pass our relay as plain text. The relay forwards them. It does not read, store or log them. The AI app’s provider sees the content under its own policy. Chats in the Pi Remote app stay end-to-end encrypted.

    Do I need the Pi Remote iPhone app?

    No. The computer app and a browser are enough. The iPhone app makes approval one tap, and lets you turn access on and off from anywhere.

    Which AI apps work?

    Claude (paid plans; the free plan allows one connector), ChatGPT (Plus and higher; add it on the web), Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and other apps that support remote MCP.

    Which model does the work?

    Your AI app’s own model. The model sign-ins in Pi Remote are not used for these calls.

    What if my computer is asleep?

    Calls fail until it is awake and online. Your AI app shows an error and you can try again.
