# Hey Atu brand

Status: 2026-10-10, **direction E chosen by the product team**: the flat dog face
from the current iOS app icon. Name, type and colour below are agreed. Concept
files only; vectors and final exports are still to do. Concepts: [`brand/concepts/`](../../brand/concepts/).

## Name and story

- **Name:** Hey Atu (Chinese: 阿图). Atu is our own Jack Russell terrier.
- **Product:** the agent runs on your own Mac; your phone is the remote.
- **Line:** *Your Mac keeps working. You go walk the dog.* / 电脑继续干活，你去遛阿图。
- **Attitude:** vibe coding as a way of living: give the time back to family and the dog.
- **Domains (not bought; RDAP showed them free on 2026-10-09):** `heyatu.com` (main),
  `atu.dog` (short link), `heyatu.app` (spare). Taken: `atu.app`, `atu.ai`, `hiatu.com`.
- **Trademark:** not searched. Search "Hey Atu" and "阿图" in classes 9 and 42 before launch.
  Known conflict risk: German A.T.U holds an "ATU" international mark that includes class 9.

## Mark family: direction E, flat Atu

Flat fills, no outlines: cream face, caramel floppy ears, caramel patch over the
viewer-right eye joined to the right ear, slanted pill eyes, peach blush.

| Mark | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| App icon (close-up crop on ink) | `atu-E-appicon.png` | iOS app icon, unchanged from the shipping app |
| Full face (transparent) | `atu-E-face.png` | Primary mark: wordmark lockups, site nav and hero, splash, share card, merch |
| Favicon (full face on ink, rounded) | `atu-E-favicon.png` | Browser tab, 16–32 px |
| Avatar (full face on ink disc) | `atu-E-avatar.png` | Social profile pictures, stickers |
| Plush Atu | `atu-plush-avatar.png` | Character only: site hero dogs, Agent Mode states, videos. Not a logo |

The icon keeps the close-up crop users already know; everything that sits next
to the wordmark uses the full face. The full face was generated from the app icon
and still needs a vector redraw that matches the icon's shapes exactly.

On cream or white backgrounds the cream face edge is soft. Where it must stand
out (favicon, avatar, small sizes), use the ink-backed versions.

Parked, not in the system: A as a logo (kept as the plush character), B
`alt-handwritten-atu.png`, C `alt-paw-doodle.png`, D `atu-J1…J3-*.png` (Jack Russell doodle).
