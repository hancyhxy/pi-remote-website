Frequently asked questions

What is Pi?

Pi is the open agent framework your AI runs on. The installer sets it up for you, or uses the Pi you already have. You do not need to know it to use Pi Remote.

Which computers are supported?

Mac and Linux (with systemd). Windows is next.

Is it only for code?

No. Use it for research, follow-ups, price and inbox watching, planning and errands, as well as code.

Which models can I use?

More than 30 providers, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi and Qwen. Sign in with a subscription you already have, or add an API key.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer: chats, to-dos, scheduled results and files. The model provider you choose still sees what your AI sends to it, under its own data policy.

Does my computer need to stay awake?

Yes. Your AI runs on your computer, so it must be awake and online to do work and send results.

Do I need Tailscale or a VPN?

No. Your computer connects out to a Cloudflare relay. You do not open any inbound port.

What does end-to-end encryption cover?

Session content between your phone and your computer. The relay sees connection metadata, but not the content.

Can I continue a session that is open in my terminal?

Yes. Your phone messages appear in the terminal, and terminal dialogs also open on the phone. The first answer wins.

How many phones can I pair?

Up to five devices for each computer. You can forget one device at a time, or all of them.

How do the trial and refund work?

The first 7 days are free. Your first payment is on day 7. If you change your mind, you get a full refund within 3 days of that payment.

Is there an App Store app?

Not yet. Pi Remote is a native iPhone and iPad app. Early access members get it first.
